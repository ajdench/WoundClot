# XStat and Surgicel

XStat and Surgicel are important hemorrhage-control adjuncts, but they are not general substitutes for hemostatic wound-packing gauze.

## XStat

XStat uses expanding mini-sponges and is best understood as a specialty device for deep, narrow-tract wounds, particularly some junctional hemorrhage scenarios. It is not a general gauze replacement.

The 2024 Heliyon rat grade-IV liver injury study found worse outcomes with XStat minisponge groups than traditional gauze packing in that model. This does not invalidate XStat's intended narrow-tract role, but it is a strong warning against extrapolating across anatomy.

## Surgicel

Surgicel is an oxidized regenerated cellulose surgical hemostat. It has a long role in surgical bleeding control, but the evidence reviewed here does not support treating it as a leading field traumatic hemorrhage gauze.

Older US Army aortotomy work and the 2024 Heliyon liver model both provide caution around oxidized cellulose products in severe traumatic bleeding models. WoundClot is a different cellulose product and should not be equated with Surgicel, but these studies reinforce the need for product-specific trauma evidence.
