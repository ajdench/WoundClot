# XStat and Surgicel

XStat and Surgicel are important hemorrhage-control adjuncts, but they are not general substitutes for hemostatic wound-packing gauze.

## XStat

XStat uses expanding mini-sponges and is best understood as a specialty device family for deep, narrow-tract wounds, particularly some junctional hemorrhage scenarios. XStat 12/30-style products are not general gauze replacements.

XStat has swine junctional and non-compressible hemorrhage literature, including comparisons against Combat Gauze and emerging injectable/expandable materials. The 2024 Heliyon rat grade-IV liver injury study found worse outcomes with XStat minisponge groups than traditional gauze packing in that model. This does not invalidate XStat's intended narrow-tract role, but it is a strong warning against extrapolating across anatomy.

## Surgicel

Surgicel is an oxidized regenerated cellulose surgical hemostat family, including sheet/fibrillar/powder-like surgical formats. It has a long role in surgical bleeding control, but the evidence reviewed here does not support treating Surgicel variants as leading field traumatic hemorrhage gauze.

Older US Army aortotomy work and the 2024 Heliyon liver model both provide caution around oxidized cellulose products in severe traumatic bleeding models. WoundClot is a different cellulose product and should not be equated with Surgicel, but these studies reinforce the need for product-specific trauma evidence.
