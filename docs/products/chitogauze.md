# ChitoGauze

ChitoGauze and ChitoGauze PRO are mature chitosan-based hemostatic gauze products.

## Position

ChitoGauze is a strong alternative in the same mature evidence/guideline ecosystem as Combat Gauze and Celox. It is relevant because it shows that the strongest comparison is not only WoundClot vs Celox, but WoundClot vs the broader CoTCCC-recognized product class.

## Mechanism

Like Celox, ChitoGauze uses chitosan's adhesive interaction with blood and tissue to support local hemostasis. This is often described as forming a mucoadhesive physical barrier that may be less dependent on the host coagulation cascade than kaolin.

## Evidence Role

The Dutch EMS 66-case ChitoGauze study is useful real-world civilian evidence. It provides a model for the kind of human trauma/EMS data WoundClot lacks.

## Limitation

ChitoGauze is not the primary focus of this WoundClot-led review, and direct clinical head-to-head evidence across all mature products remains limited.
