# ChitoGauze XR PRO

ChitoGauze XR PRO is a mature chitosan-based hemostatic gauze product and deserves a more specific position than generic "chitosan gauze."

## Position

ChitoGauze XR PRO is a strong alternative in the same mature evidence/guideline ecosystem as Combat Gauze and Celox. It is relevant because it shows that the strongest comparison is not only WoundClot vs Celox, but WoundClot vs the broader CoTCCC-recognized product class.

Tricol Biomedical describes ChitoGauze XR PRO as a flexible chitosan-coated gauze dressing for external temporary control of severely bleeding wounds, with an x-ray-detectable thread and CoTCCC-approved status. Product details reviewed list a 3 in x 4 yd Z-fold format, latex-free packaging, and NSN 6510-01-591-7740.

FDA 510(k) K153582 identifies Prometheus ChitoGauze XR PRO as a chitosan wound dressing. The summary describes a polyester/rayon non-woven gauze with radiopaque filament, coated with chitosan, z-folded and terminally sterilized. Its indication is external, temporary control of severely bleeding wounds.

## Mechanism

Like Celox, ChitoGauze XR PRO uses chitosan's adhesive interaction with blood and tissue to support local hemostasis. This is often described as forming a mucoadhesive physical barrier that may be less dependent on the host coagulation cascade than kaolin.

## Evidence Role

The Dutch EMS 66-case ChitoGauze study is useful real-world civilian evidence. It provides a model for the kind of human trauma/EMS data WoundClot lacks.

FDA K153582 reports non-clinical performance testing including biocompatibility, radiopacity, and in vivo efficacy. Two swine studies are described: a 6 mm femoral perforation injury model for the 4 in x 4 yd product size and a splenic capsular strip injury model for a smaller dressing; in both, the device controlled bleeding at least as well as the competitive reference product. The FDA summary states no clinical data were required for that device evaluation.

ChitoGauze XR PRO also appears in a coagulopathic swine junctional hemorrhage comparison alongside Combat Gauze, Celox Rapid, ChitoSAM, XStat 30, EVARREST, TAC Wrapping Gauze, and BloodSTOP IX. In that study, most agents, including ChitoGauze XR PRO, showed no difference from Combat Gauze in tested efficiency parameters, while BloodSTOP IX performed worse. This makes XR PRO stronger than WoundClot in the general comparison because it has field-relevant animal comparison data against the benchmark product.

## Limitation

ChitoGauze XR PRO is not the primary focus of this WoundClot-led review, and direct clinical head-to-head evidence across all mature products remains limited. The strongest product-specific human trauma evidence in this wiki remains stronger for Celox than for ChitoGauze XR PRO, but XR PRO belongs clearly above WoundClot in the current field-trauma evidence hierarchy.
