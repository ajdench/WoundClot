# Plain Gauze

Plain gauze is not an active hemostatic product, but it is central to major hemorrhage control.

## Position

Plain gauze is the baseline comparator and a practical essential. Correct wound packing and sustained direct pressure can outperform advanced products used badly.

## Mechanism

Plain gauze works mechanically:

- fills wound space;
- places pressure directly on the bleeding source;
- provides surface area for clot formation;
- supports pressure-dressing application after packing.

## Evidence Role

Many studies use plain gauze or standard pressure dressing as the control. The 2024 Heliyon rat liver-injury study found traditional gauze packing outperformed XStat minisponge and XStat plus Surgicel in that internal solid-organ model.

## Limitation

When technique is equal and the wound is appropriate, mature hemostatic gauze can outperform plain gauze. Plain gauze should not be treated as equivalent to Combat Gauze/Celox/ChitoGauze for every severe bleeding scenario.
