# QuikClot Combat Gauze Variants

QuikClot Combat Gauze is the benchmark hemostatic gauze in this review. The field-comparable variants include standard Combat Gauze Z-fold, Combat Gauze XL, and TraumaPad where a pad format is appropriate. QuikClot Control+ is a surgical/internal bleeding product and should not be treated as the same evidence category as prehospital wound-packing Combat Gauze.

## Position

QuikClot Combat Gauze is the CoTCCC/TCCC hemostatic dressing of choice for compressible external hemorrhage not amenable to limb tourniquet use, with Celox Gauze and ChitoGauze-family variants listed as alternatives in TCCC materials reviewed here.

## Mechanism

QuikClot Combat Gauze is kaolin-impregnated gauze. Kaolin activates the intrinsic clotting pathway, especially factor XII, and supports clot formation when the gauze is properly packed against the bleeding source with sustained pressure.

## Evidence Role

QuikClot Combat Gauze is often the comparator in animal models and systematic reviews. IDF evidence found in this review also points to QuikClot Combat Gauze rather than WoundClot or Celox.

Additional relevant evidence includes swine work in hypothermic/hemodiluted models and movement/rebleeding scenarios. Those studies are important because real prehospital care involves transport, movement, resuscitation, and physiologic clotting impairment, not just static initial hemostasis.

## Limitation

Its benchmark role does not mean it is intrinsically superior in every wound or patient. Severe coagulopathy, training, packing quality, and wound anatomy still matter.
